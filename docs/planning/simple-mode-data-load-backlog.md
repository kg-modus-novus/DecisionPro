**Research notes**

Travel measures use unit 'hours' (estimated drive hours; x60 for minutes); rows carry straightLineMiles. HPSA rows: value = highest area HPSA score, with designationType and coverage fields. Provider-ratio rows are per 10,000 residents (unit 'ratio'). Core Set 2025 reflects mostly CY2024 service dates. SDUD totals exclude CMS-suppressed small rows. County member counts for the PCP estimate come from the repo's countyAccessContext.js (DMS county PDF 2026-07), not re-fetched. The hand-entered SFY2024 BH-by-plan figures in reviewData.js match slide 11 of the Feb 2025 DMS deck (the deck cover is dated Feb 5, 2025; LRC filed it under the Feb 12, 2025 meeting). Single PBM context: MedImpact has processed pharmacy claims and prior authorizations for all MCOs since July 1, 2021 using the FFS preferred drug list (MedImpact/CHFS provider mailing, April 1, 2021, https://www.chfs.ky.gov/agencies/dms/dpo/ppb/Documents/ProviderMailingApril2021Final.pdf). 988 KY in-state answer rate was 90-91% every month Jan-Aug 2026; no statutory target, the Lifeline report bands >=90% as top tier.

**Research notes**

Periods: CMS-64/MACPAC/KFF figures are federal fiscal years (Oct-Sep); Kentucky budget and DMS deck figures are state fiscal years (Jul-Jun). CMS-64 amounts are net expenditures reported for federal match in the year, including prior-period adjustments. MACStats Exhibit 17 'Institutional LTSS' ($1,755M FFY 2024) = nursing facility ($1,555.5M) + ICF/IID ($168.1M) + mental health facility ($31.4M); MACPAC and KFF reconcile. Directed payments (HRIP, university) flow through MCO capitation, so they sit inside the CMS-64 managed care category. KHA's $2.2B HRIP figure is from an advocacy presentation.
